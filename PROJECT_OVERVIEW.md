# Project Overview

## The Problem
Anxious thoughts often demand immediate attention, pulling focus from daily tasks and spiraling into rumination. Traditional advice to simply "stop worrying" is largely ineffective and can exacerbate the problem.

## The Solution
Worry Box provides a structured, psychological release valve. By typing a worry and visually "locking" it away, the user engages in **worry postponement**. 

## Psychological Basis
This app is rooted in CBT principles:
- **Acknowledge:** The worry is recognized, not ignored.
- **Delay:** The brain is told "not now, later," which is easier to accept than "never."
- **Re-evaluate:** When the scheduled time arrives, the worry has often lost its emotional charge.

## Goals
- Provide a simple, frictionless user experience.
- Maintain total privacy (all data is local).
- Deliver a tangible sense of relief through design and interaction.
